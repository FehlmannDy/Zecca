# Zecca — demo script

**Live:** https://zecca-beta.vercel.app (web, Vercel) · API https://zecca-api-production.up.railway.app (Railway) · XRPL **Devnet** for every payment, **Testnet** + Flare **Coston2** for top-up and Earn.
Everything a judge needs is in the browser. Use two devices (or two browser profiles): one is the customer, one is the merchant.
On in production: top-up (MoonPay), card, Earn, metals, identity, statements, the demo shop, Face ID and Google sign-in. Withdrawal (MoonPay sell) is off.

## One MoonPay sandbox purchase

On https://zecca-beta.vercel.app, signed in (a fresh account with its $50 demo credit is fine):

1. **More → Add money**, pick **$25** (MoonPay's minimum is about €21.60), **Continue**. MoonPay's widget opens over the page; the screen says "Sandbox purchase. No real money moved." From an EU/EEA IP the widget offers XRP instead of RLUSD (see step 4).
2. In the widget: an email address you can read (MoonPay sends a code to it), the code, then a UK address.
3. Pay with MoonPay's sandbox test card `4485 0403 7153 6584`, expiry `12/2030`, CVC `123`, and confirm.
4. MoonPay delivers RLUSD on XRPL Testnet, or XRP from an EU/EEA IP such as Italy, where MoonPay does not offer RLUSD on the XRP Ledger; Zecca credits either after checking the delivery: it checks the `Payment` to its treasury on the ledger, values what was delivered (RLUSD one to one, XRP at the price MoonPay charged for this purchase), multiplies it by the sandbox multiplier (`RAMP_SANDBOX_SCALE`, 100 in production), mints that into the pool on Devnet and pays it out to you.
5. About 30 s later the screen says "Added $X." and home shows a Top up row marked Sandbox.

## What the public ledger sees

Addresses: yes. Amounts: no. Who paid whom: no — every payment goes _payer → Zecca pool_ and _pool → recipient_; the link lives only in Zecca's database. Timing can still link a deposit to its payout (stated plainly on `/how-it-works`).

## If something goes wrong during the demo

- Devnet slow or "not synced": wait 30 s and retry; the API retries itself.
- No email code: use "Continue with Google".
- Card declined everywhere: the Lithic webhook must point at the API (`services/api/src/connectors/card/cli.ts enroll --base https://zecca-api-production.up.railway.app`).
- Local fallback, no network: `npm run dev` with `LEDGER_MODE=mock SIGNER_MODE=mock` runs the whole demo on the mock ledger (labelled).
